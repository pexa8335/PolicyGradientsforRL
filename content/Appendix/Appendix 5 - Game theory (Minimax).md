# Definition.

[Minimax for Two Players Games](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/eroberts/courses/soco/projects/1998-99/game-theory/Minimax.html)

In a two-person, zero-sum game, a person can win only if the other player loses. No cooperation is possible.
